# Customer letter

*In the customer's voice. Written before any code, to keep us honest about who this is for.*

---

Hi,

I run a small home goods brand on Amazon. Candles, towels, a couple of puzzles, some pet stuff. Around forty SKUs. Most of it goes FBA through a prep centre we pay per unit, and a few orders ship from our 3PL. It's me and one ops person. That's the whole team.

Every month I download the settlement report on a Sunday and scroll through it with a coffee, and every month there are lines I can't explain. An inbound defect fee on a shipment from six weeks ago. A unit marked "lost inbound" with nothing paid back. A fulfilment fee that puts a candle set in a heavier tier than I think it should be.

None of them is big. That's the problem. Each one is too small to spend an afternoon on, so I let it go. But I added them up once for a quarter and didn't like the number.

When I have tried to dispute, Seller Support asks for proof. And that's where it falls apart. The prep photos are on someone's phone at the prep centre. The receiving count is in a sheet that nobody updated after the pallet came in. I know the label was fine. I can't show it.

I used an agency for a few months. They took a cut and filed everything they could find. A lot got rejected, and I started worrying that all those weak claims were making my account look bad. I stopped.

What I actually want is simple:

- Show me which charges my own records prove wrong, and show me the records.
- Give me the amount and how you worked it out, so I can check it.
- If you're not sure, just say you're not sure. Tell me what's missing so we start recording it next time.
- Don't hand me a claim you can't back up. I'd honestly rather miss three good claims than file one bad one.

If you can do that, I'll trust what you give me. If you can't, tell me that too.

Thanks,
A seller who's tired of guessing
